Interactive creation with ClikB

Build interactive visual experiences with ClikB using scene nodes, branching choices, hotspots and spatial workflows.

Create scenes people can explore

Describe an interactive idea and develop it into scene nodes. Branching choices can lead to different outcomes, while hotspots connect interactions to the experience.

Spatial interactions

Build connected stages and scene nodes with configured movement paths, action clips and links between stages. Viewers use directional controls to move along those paths, trigger available actions and return to previous nodes or stages. A visual map helps viewers explore the connections. Preview the spatial experience before publishing.

Swipe and touch hotspots

Connect video interactions through swipe or touch hotspots. Configure which interaction leads to which scene or outcome, letting viewers explore a branching experience.

An example

A character approaches a horse. One branch can show the character reaching and riding it; another can show the horse escaping. The scene images and interaction configuration work together to express those outcomes.

Review before publishing

Refine the images and configure the interactions for the selected workflow. Preview the experience before publishing it for viewers to explore.
