AI video creation with ClikB

Create short videos and cinematic stories from your ideas with ClikB scene planning, image editing, video generation and audio tools.

Turn an idea into visual scenes

Describe the experience you want to make. ClikB prepares scene content and image prompts that you can review and refine before generating clips.

Shorts and Cinema

Use Shorts for short-form visual content or Cinema for a sequence of paired Stage A and Stage B scenes. Your prompt can describe a story, recap, explanation, demonstration or music-led idea.

Shape the finished video

Edit scene images and prompts, generate clips and work with narration and music where supported by your selected workflow. Optional supporting shots are user-directed additions.

Visual prompts and Three.js motion references

Use a visual image prompt to guide appearance, or build and edit a Three.js Motion Guide for one-shot choreography, subject movement, relative positions, timing and camera direction. Preview and export the guide as a reference video before generating the finished shot with a compatible AI video model.
