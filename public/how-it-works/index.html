How ClikB works

From your first prompt to scenes, images, video, audio and interactive playback: learn the ClikB creation workflow.

1. Describe your idea

Enter a request and select a creation mode. Add details such as style, characters, references or your own scenes when useful.

Conversational help during creation

A project-aware conversational AI concierge can help you explore ideas, understand the current creation and refine your direction throughout the workflow.

2. Review the scene plan

ClikB develops your request into scenes and prepares visual direction. Cinema uses paired Stage A and Stage B depictions for each logical scene.

3. Create and refine

Review scene images and edit prompts. Depending on the workflow, add generated video, narration, music or optional user-directed supporting shots.

4. Publish and experience

Publish or render your creation, then watch it or interact with it in the player. Interactive workflows can use hotspots, branching scenes or spatial configuration.
